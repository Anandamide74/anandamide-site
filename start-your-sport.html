The Science of Progress

Periodisation —
Training with a plan.

Most people train randomly — same workout, same intensity, week after week — and then wonder why they plateau. Periodisation is the science of structuring training into phases so your body peaks exactly when you need it to. Every Anandamide programme is periodised — whether you're preparing for a marathon, a tournament, or simply your best physical self.

PHASE 01
→
Foundation
Weeks 1–4 · Base Building
Build the aerobic base, movement quality and structural integrity. Low intensity, high volume. The phase most people skip — and then get injured.
  • Aerobic capacity
  • Mobility & movement patterns
  • Injury resilience
  • Habit formation
PHASE 02
→
Build
Weeks 5–10 · Strength & Conditioning
Increase intensity and sport-specific load. Strength, speed and skill development. Mental training begins — focus, pressure management, self-regulation.
  • Sport-specific strength
  • Speed & power
  • Mental skills training
  • Nutrition optimisation
PHASE 03
→
Peak
Weeks 11–14 · Competition Ready
Sharpen everything. Reduce volume, increase intensity and specificity. This is where your training produces peak physical and mental performance. Race day. Match day. Your day.
  • Peak performance output
  • Competition simulation
  • Pressure & focus mastery
  • Race/match day preparation
PHASE 04
Recovery
Weeks 15–16 · Regeneration
Structured recovery is training. The phase that lets you come back stronger for the next cycle. Active recovery, movement, nutrition, sleep and nervous system reset.
  • Physical regeneration
  • Nervous system recovery
  • Review & recalibrate
  • Next cycle planning

Timelines vary by sport, goal and individual. All programmes are fully personalised.
